A screen that melts into the glass.

Three lenses. One continuous plateau.

A full-width camera plateau holds the lenses, flash, microphone and LiDAR scanner, finished in the same tone as the titanium frame.

Power that stays out of the way.

A large L-shaped cell, a Taptic Engine for precise haptics and a MagSafe coil array that snaps on and charges without cables.

Taptic EngineClean, precise haptics for every tap.
